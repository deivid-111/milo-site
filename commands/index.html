Commands

All of them are slash commands and work inside a server. Options in [brackets] are optional.

For everyone

CommandWhat it does
/claimClaim miles with the code your crew gave your cabin
/miles [member]Profile card: balance, lifetime miles, tier, rank and progress
/history [member]The last 10 changes to your miles, with reasons
/leaderboard [page]Top flyers in this airline by lifetime miles
/achievements [member]What's unlocked, and progress on the rest
/shopRewards you can spend miles on
/redeemSpend miles on a reward
/flight infoWho was on a flight, their cabin and miles
/route checkHow many miles a route earns in each cabin
/route listRoutes with custom miles, and what the rest earn
/setup viewThis server's settings (only you see it)
/dailyDaily check-in for bonus miles, if the airline turned it on
/event statusWhether a bonus miles event is running
/alliance infoThe alliance this airline is in
/premiumWhat Premium includes, and how to get it
/privacySee or delete what milo keeps about you
/helpHelp, by topic

For staff

Staff means the role from /setup staff-role, or anyone with Manage Server.

CommandWhat it does
/flight logOpen a flight for claims and get one code per cabin
/flight codesShow a flight's codes again
/flight closeStop claims early
/flight addPut someone on a flight by hand, even after claims close
/flight removeTake someone off a flight and take back their miles
/flight voidCancel a flight and take back everyone's miles
/route setFixed miles for one route, both directions
/route removePut a route back to the normal rule
/adjust addGive a member miles, with a reason
/adjust removeTake miles away, with a reason
/reward addAdd a reward to the shop, with optional stock
/reward removeTake a reward out of the shop
/redemptions pendingReward claims still waiting
/redemptions fulfilMark a claim as given
/redemptions refundCancel a claim and give the miles back

For admins

Anyone with Manage Server.

CommandWhat it does
/setup staff-rolePick the role that counts as staff
/setup log-channelWhere milo posts flights, rewards and notices
/setup earningDistance or flat miles, cabin multipliers, claim window
/tiers thresholdHow many lifetime miles a tier needs
/tiers roleA role members get when they reach a tier

Premium Premium

These need Premium on the server, and most need Manage Server. What's Premium?

CommandWhat it does
/setup wizardSet everything up step by step
/setup dailyTurn on /daily, with a streak bonus
/branding name, color, logoYour airline's name, color and logo on milo's messages
/branding footerShow or hide the Powered by milo line
/branding nicknamemilo's nickname in your server
/branding emoji set, resetSwap milo's emojis for your own
/branding resetBack to milo's own look
/tiers rename, add, removeYour own tiers, up to 10
/achievement add, edit, remove, resetYour own achievements, up to 40
/event start, stopBonus miles for a while, like a Double Miles Weekend
/alliance create, join, leaveTeam up with up to 5 other airlines
/alliance rate, code, kickRun your alliance, if you lead it
crew on /flight logMention up to 15 crew and they earn miles too
/custombot setup, status, removeRun milo as your own bot (Custom Bot add-on)

Nothing matches that. Try /help in Discord, or ask in the support server.
