Custom Bot

milo, but it's your airline's bot. Your name, your avatar, same miles and commands underneath.

Custom Bot is an add-on for Premium airlines. You make a bot in the Discord Developer Portal, give milo its token, and from then on your bot answers /claim, /miles and everything else in your server.

Your passengers' miles, flights and settings carry straight over, so nothing restarts. Once your bot joins, milo leaves the server so commands don't show up twice.

Custom Bot
300 / month

On top of Premium. Not part of the free trial.

Setting it up

  1. Subscribe to Custom Bot in the milo Shop game (16+, a Roblox rule for new games) and claim it the same way as Premium, with Claim Premium on /premium. Your server needs Premium as well.
  2. Run /custombot setup. It lists the steps for making the bot in the Developer Portal and has an Enter token button for when you're done.
  3. Add your bot with the button milo gives you. That's it.

/custombot status tells you whether it's online, and /custombot remove switches you back to milo whenever you like. Miles and settings stay either way.

Good to know

  • If you cancel, your bot keeps running until the end of the month you paid for. Then it posts a link to invite milo back and goes offline. It comes back by itself if you renew.
  • If you reset your bot's token in the Developer Portal, milo stops it and won't keep retrying. Run /custombot setup again with the new token.
  • Your bot runs alongside milo, so if milo is ever down for maintenance, yours is too.
