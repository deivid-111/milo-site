What milo does

Everything on this page is free. The Premium extras are on their own page.

Flights and claim codes

There are no scheduled flights to set up. You fly in PTFS like you always do, and afterwards a staff member logs it with /flight log. Any real airport works: type JFK, KJFK or just search "Heathrow".

milo replies privately with a code for each cabin and posts a "flight landed" message so passengers know to claim. Claims stay open for 60 minutes unless you change it, per flight or for the whole server.

Every flight gets an ID, and staff use it to fix things:

If...Use
you lost the codes/flight codes
someone missed the window/flight add
someone claimed who wasn't there/flight remove
the flight shouldn't count/flight void
you want to see who was on it/flight info
Screenshot: staff view after /flight log, with the three codes

Can people cheat with codes?

A bit, and it's worth being honest about. A code works for anyone who has it until claims close, so a passenger could pass it to a friend. Check /flight info after a flight and remove anyone who wasn't on board, and keep the window short. What codes do stop is cabin swapping and guessing: each cabin has its own code, and each person gets 5 wrong tries a minute.

If you'd rather skip codes completely, log the flight and add each passenger yourself with /flight add.

How miles are worked out

By default a flight earns one mile per mile flown, using the great-circle distance between the two airports. Then the cabin multiplier kicks in: Economy x1, Business x1.5, First x2.

LHR to JFK in Business is 3,442 x 1.5, so about 5,163 miles.

Prefer a flat amount per flight? /setup earning switches modes and changes the multipliers. /route set gives one route a fixed amount in both directions, which is handy for short hops you want to be worth more. Staff can also give or take miles by hand with /adjust, and the reason shows up in the member's history.

Tiers

Every member has a balance (what they can spend) and lifetime miles (everything they've ever earned). Tiers go by lifetime, so spending in the shop never drops anyone a tier.

Change the numbers with /tiers threshold, and hand out a Discord role at each tier with /tiers role.

The reward shop

Staff add rewards with a price in miles and, if you like, a stock limit ("5 first class upgrades"). Members browse with /shop and buy with /redeem.

milo doesn't hand the reward over itself, because a reward can be anything: a role, an upgrade on the next flight, a shout-out. Instead it posts the claim in your log channel, pings staff, and adds a Mark as given button for whoever sorts it out. Need to undo one? /redemptions refund gives the miles back.

Profile cards and the leaderboard

/miles draws an image card with someone's balance, lifetime miles, flights, tier, progress to the next tier and rank. /leaderboard ranks everyone in your airline by lifetime miles, and /history shows the last ten things that changed their miles.

Privacy

milo doesn't read your messages and doesn't share or sell anything. Anyone can run /privacy to see what milo keeps about them and delete it, in one server or everywhere.
