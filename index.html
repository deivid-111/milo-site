Give your PTFS airline a mileage program.

milo is a Discord bot. After a flight your staff get one claim code per cabin, passengers type it in Discord, and the miles go straight onto their profile. They climb tiers, unlock achievements and spend what they earn in a shop you stock.

How a flight works

  1. Your flight lands in PTFS. Someone on staff runs /flight log with the flight number and the two airports.
  2. milo hands them three codes, one each for Economy, Business and First. Only they see them.
  3. Staff give out the codes however suits you: in game chat, a passengers channel, anywhere.
  4. Passengers run /claim. The code decides the cabin, so nobody gets First on an Economy ticket.

What your passengers get

Every claim shows what it earned, and tiers and achievements pop up the moment they're unlocked. Miles are based on the real distance between the two airports, so a long haul is worth more than a hop. Buenos Aires to Frankfurt is about 7.146 miles, and Business and First pay 1.5x and 2x.

  • A profile card with their balance, tier and rank (/miles)
  • Tiers from Bronze up to Platinum, with a Discord role at each one if you want
  • Nine achievements to start, like Long haul and Globetrotter
  • A leaderboard for your airline
  • A reward shop where they spend miles on things you hand out

Setting it up

  1. /setup staff-role picks who can log flights and fix miles.
  2. /setup log-channel picks a staff channel where milo posts every flight and reward claim.
  3. /reward add puts something in the shop, so miles are worth chasing.

That's all you have to do. If a flight goes wrong later, there's a fix for it: /flight add for someone who missed the claim window, /flight remove for someone who wasn't on board, and /flight void to cancel the whole thing and take the miles back.
