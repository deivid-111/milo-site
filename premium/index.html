What you get

Your airline's look

Your name, logo and color on every message and profile card, your own emojis in place of milo's, a nickname for the bot in your server, and no "Powered by milo" line. It's all under /branding.

Events

/event start multiplies the miles on every flight for a set time. A 2x Double Miles Weekend is the classic one, but anything from 1.1x to 5x works, for up to two weeks.

Alliances

Team up with up to five other Premium airlines. When someone flies with one of you, they also get partner miles (50% by default) at every other airline in the alliance they're a member of.

Daily check-in

Members run /daily once a day for bonus miles, with extra for each day in a row, up to a week.

Crew miles

Mention your crew when you log a flight and they earn miles too, with their own multiplier.

Your own tiers and achievements

Rename the tiers, add up to 10, and write up to 40 achievements of your own.

And a couple of smaller things

/setup wizard walks you through setup, and on the 1st of every month your log channel gets a report with last month's flights, miles, top flyers and busiest routes.

How to get it

  1. Link your Roblox account. Run /premium in your server and press Link Roblox. Type the code it gives you into the Discord tab in the milo Shop game.
  2. Buy a plan in the game, or start the free trial from the Airlines tab.
  3. Turn it on for your airline. Press Turn on Premium here in the game's Airlines tab, or Claim Premium on /premium in Discord. You need Manage Server in that airline.

Can't join the game? Link Roblox also has a Sign in with Roblox button that works without it.

When Premium ends

  • milo warns you in your log channel 3 days before.
  • Everything goes back to milo's own look, but your branding, tiers and emojis are saved and come back if you renew.
  • Nobody loses any miles.

Moving a subscription to a different server? Ask in the support server.
